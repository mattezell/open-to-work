# OPEN TO WORK - design

A 16-bit co-op beat 'em up about the job hunt. Player 1 is Matt, a bearded
architect laid off on a Tuesday. Player 2 is an AI sidekick that fights beside
him: strong, fast, and occasionally confidently wrong. Built Tuesday to Friday
(2026-09-22 to 2026-09-25) as a TNG Deep Dive: "Laid off Tuesday, shipped by
Friday."

## Pillars

1. **Readable in five seconds.** Walk right, hit things, feel good. Genesis
   palette, chunky outlines, big hit-stop.
2. **Human plus AI, as a mechanic.** The sidekick clears crowds on its own,
   but it is only great when you direct it. That is the thesis of the piece,
   made playable.
3. **Punch at the absurdity, never at people.** Enemies are the hiring
   process (ATS bots, spam recruiters, whiteboard puzzles), never an employer
   or a real person. His former employer is not in the game.
4. **Everything generated from source in the repo.** Sprites from a manifest
   via codex imagegen, audio synthesized, banter generated by a local model at
   build time. "Can we ship this" is always yes.

## Core loop

Belt-scrolling stage (x plus depth lane, Streets of Rage style). Enemies spawn
in waves; the camera locks until the wave is clear, then "GO ->". Health
pickups (Coffee) and a Referral power-up. Three stages, a boss, an ending.

## Controls (keyboard; gamepad if time allows)

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrows / WASD | Move (x and depth) |
| J | Attack (3-hit chain: jab, jab, haymaker) |
| K | Jump (jump attack with J) |
| L | Special: 360 clear, costs a sliver of health (SoR rule) |
| Walk into a stunned enemy | Grab; J to knee, direction + J to throw |
| 1 / 2 / 3 | Direct the sidekick: Focus my target / Guard me / Go wild |

## The sidekick

- Computer-controlled from the same simulation as the player, so it obeys the
  same hitboxes and rules.
- **Default mode** is "Go wild": high damage, but it chases the biggest crowd
  and sometimes swings at a Ghosting Phantom that already left
  ("hallucinated target" whiff with a sheepish bark).
- **Directed modes** make it much stronger: Focus doubles its damage on your
  target, Guard intercepts attacks aimed at you.
- **Banter** comes from a bark bank of short lines per event (wave clear,
  low health, whiff, player KO, boss intro), generated ahead of time by
  qwen3.8-27b on a local model server and committed as JSON. No runtime LLM calls:
  the public build cannot reach the box, and a live model in a public game is
  a prompt-injection and cost surface for no gameplay gain.
- Name and look: open question (see below).

## Stages

1. **The Job Board.** A downtown street and a job fair. Enemies: ATS Bots
   (walk up and shred your resume), Spam Recruiters (keep their distance and
   throw "5 years of experience in a 2-year-old framework" cards along their
   depth line; step off the line or jump). Boss: The Unpaid Take Home, armored
   while it swings, calling in scope creep (one ATS Bot) at two thirds and
   one third health. Coffee pickups heal. As built: see JOURNAL, M4 Stage 1.
2. **The Take-Home Tunnel.** A Battletoads turbo-tunnel homage: an
   autoscrolling hover-bike run dodging walls of requirements. Short, hard,
   with checkpoints (unlike the original).
3. **Interview Tower.** LeetCode Golems (armored, must be thrown), Ghosting
   Phantoms (fade out mid-fight). Boss: **The Panel**, a long desk with three
   interviewer heads that attack in rotation. "FINISH HIM" on the last head;
   the finisher is handing over the offer letter. Ending: HIRED.

## Architecture

- **Phaser 3.90 + TypeScript + Vite + Vitest**, same stack as Craft Beer Hero.
- **Sim-first**: all game rules live in `src/sim/` as pure, deterministic,
  engine-agnostic TypeScript (fixed 60 Hz tick, seeded RNG, input as data).
  Phaser only renders and reads input.
- **Beatability proof in CI** (the Momentum standard): a scripted bot drives
  the sim headless through every stage and must clear it within a time and
  health budget. A stage that cannot be beaten does not ship.
- **Deploy**: Cloudflare Workers static assets plus a small Worker API for a
  leaderboard (KV), following Momentum's `worker/` layout. Score validation
  by replaying the input log through the sim server-side, like Momentum's
  anti-cheat. The leaderboard is a Friday stretch goal, not a blocker.

## Asset pipeline

- `tools/assets.yaml` manifest: a `style` block (world paragraph, sprite
  suffix, a verbatim character description per actor) and one entry per
  sheet. Pattern and gotchas: projects wiki,
  `patterns/llm-generated-game-assets.md`, and an earlier game's `tools/`.
- `codex exec` with the built-in image_gen tool, transparent background,
  stdin from /dev/null, never `--ephemeral`, and keep every raw render in
  `tools/_staging/` so post-processing can be redone without regenerating.
- Post-process: detect alpha first and only chroma-key when there is none
  (border flood), largest-blob per frame, one scale factor per sheet, one
  anchor, BOX downsample, snap to a shared palette slice.
- Player 1 is generated from a reference photo of Matt (his consent is on
  record in the opus55 session, 2026-09-22). The photo goes to OpenAI through
  his own Codex login.
- Fallback if frame consistency is too poor for fighting animation:
  procedural pixel routines, as in Craft Beer Hero's `src/game/art/`.

## Audio

Synthesized at runtime with WebAudio, as in Momentum: a funk bass line in the
ToeJam and Earl spirit, punchy hit SFX, speed-up music on the tunnel.
